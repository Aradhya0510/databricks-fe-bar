# PLAY-PROMO-004 — Promotions, Events and SNAP Playbook

**Owner:** Merchandising Planning · **Version:** 2016.1
*Fictitious internal document created for the OSA prototype.*

## 1. Calendar events
The planning calendar flags sporting, cultural, national and religious events (e.g., SuperBowl, Easter, Memorial Day,
Father's Day, Independence Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas).

* **Pre-build window:** for national events, raise order-up-to levels **5 days before** the event.
* **Uplift guidance:** FOODS_3 (snacks & beverages) +20%, FOODS_1 (fresh & dairy) +10%, HOBBIES_2 (party & seasonal) +35%
  for the event week.
* **Memorial Day** (last Monday of May): prioritise beverages, chips and grilling-adjacent pantry items; all
  critical alerts in FOODS_3 during the pre-build window must be approved, not rejected, unless delisted.

## 2. SNAP (Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program) days
SNAP benefit days differ by state and occur on 10 days each month. Food demand on SNAP days is materially higher,
especially in FOODS_2 and FOODS_3.

* Increase FOODS order quantities by **15%** for orders landing in a SNAP window.
* Do not schedule cycle counts in FOODS aisles on the first two SNAP days.

## 3. Price changes
When the shelf price drops by more than **10%** week-over-week, treat it like a promotion: expect demand uplift and
review the order-up-to level manually.

## 4. Post-event review
Within one week after each national event, Merchandising Planning reviews lost sales by department and shares
learnings in the weekly ops memo.
