**Owner:** COO Office · **Version:** FY2016

## 1. KPI targets (FY2016)
| KPI | Definition | Target |
|---|---|---|
| On-shelf availability (OSA) | share of active store-item-days not out of stock | **≥ 96.0%** |
| Lost sales rate | estimated lost revenue ÷ (revenue + lost revenue) | **< 1.5%** |
| Supplier on-time | POs received on/before expected date | **≥ 95%** |
| Alert response | Critical alerts actioned within 4 business hours | **≥ 90%** |
| Inventory cover | median days of cover across store-items | **10–16 days** |
